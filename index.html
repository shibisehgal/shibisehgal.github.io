Shibani Sehgal

Expressing the emotions of nature, in colour and form.

I'm an artist, illustrator and art experimentalist. I work in soft pastels, pen-and-ink, collage and large canvas, and I've shown in group and solo exhibitions across India and abroad. My work also lives in the collections of several eminent personalities, including the former Vice President of India.

A continuing adventure with colour

As an artist, illustrator and art experimentalist, I exhibit versatility and flexibility in my continuing adventure with colours and various media. I hope to inspire everyone to look towards art as a means of expression that transcends language and extends into the realm of unfettered freedom of exploration.

I graduated from the College of Art, New Delhi (1992) and earned a Masters in History of Art from the National Museum Institute (1995), where I was awarded the gold medal for my research.
